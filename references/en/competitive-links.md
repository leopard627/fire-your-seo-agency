# Competitors, Keyword Gaps & Backlinks — Evidence-Based Audit

Separate differences in public content from differences measured in search or link data.
Without tools or account access, continue the public audit without inventing metrics.

## 1. Comparison scope and inputs

- [ ] Select 2–5 competitors, core products/services, and customer questions.
- [ ] Distinguish direct business competitors from informational sites competing in search.
- [ ] Record country/region, language, device, engine, search type, and date range.
      Compare matching conditions; disclose any conditions that cannot be aligned.
- [ ] Record source, export date, filters, row limits, and domain/subpath coverage.
- [ ] Use existing materials and authorized connections. Without account access, start
      with public pages and user-provided Search Console/SEO-tool exports.

## 2. Public content gaps

1. Pair your URLs with competitor URLs for representative customer questions.
2. Observe titles, answers, prices/terms, comparisons, cases, authors, sources, dates,
   FAQs, and paths to the next action.
3. Identify unanswered questions and opportunities for first-hand evidence or data.
4. Choose whether to improve, consolidate, or create a page; assign a target URL.

**Public content comparison does not measure search volume, traffic, or ranking gaps.**
For search-result samples, record time, location, device, and only results actually seen.
One absent result does not prove deindexing, zero traffic, or a particular ranking range.
Do not copy competitor text/images or mass-produce thin pages for the same question.

## 3. Data-based keyword gaps

- [ ] Inspect queries, pages, clicks, impressions, CTR, and average position for your site.
      The [Search Console performance report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/7576553?hl=en)
      covers the selected property; it does not expose competitors' private performance.
- [ ] Attribute competitor metrics to available sources such as SEO-tool exports.
      Separate estimated volume/traffic from observations reported by Search Console.
- [ ] Align country, language, device, period, and search type. Where no language filter
      exists, classify page/query language separately and disclose the method and limits.
- [ ] Do not replace missing queries with zero. Check sampling, privacy, and row limits.
- [ ] Split branded/non-branded queries and informational/comparison/transactional intent;
      map each group to a URL.
- [ ] Prioritize business relevance, observed demand, and feasible page improvements.
      Without demand data, label prioritization as editorial judgment, not a numeric ranking.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|

## 4. Backlink profile

- [ ] Read user-provided Search Console Links reports or available tool exports.
      [Google's Links report](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/9049606)
      is a sample rather than a complete inventory and can include links no longer present.
- [ ] Preserve source URL, target URL, anchor, supplied discovery/check dates, and tool.
      Distinguish link rows from domain aggregates and state deduplication rules.
- [ ] Look for overlooked landing pages, concentration in a few domains, and relevant mentions.
      Report link/referring-domain counts **only within the supplied dataset's scope**.
- [ ] Open important public samples to verify the link, anchor, `rel`, and target response.
      Do not infer unreported `nofollow` attributes or whether a link has been removed.
- [ ] For broken target URLs, check for a genuine successor and propose a repair.
      Do not redirect all targets to an unrelated page or the homepage.
- [ ] Align coverage, tool, and deduplication when comparing exports over time.
      A link absent from a later sample is not confirmed lost without source-page evidence.

## 5. Execution boundaries and results

- A toxicity score or unfamiliar domain alone does not establish a penalty.
- Do not make automatic disavow-list creation or submission a default action.
  Follow [Google's disavow guidance](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/2648487?hl=en):
  review separately only with evidence of substantial artificial links and a manual action
  or a likelihood of one.
- Do not propose ranking-manipulation link purchases, automated link creation, or excessive
  exchanges contrary to [Google's link-spam policy](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies#link-spam).
- Label results `verified / estimated (named source) / unverified`, with evidence and dates.
  Leave manual-action status unverified without access to the relevant dashboard.
- Perform requested analysis and fixes within existing authorization.
  An audit request alone does not authorize contacting others or changing external accounts.
- Deliver top content gaps, sampled link issues, priorities, target URLs, and recheck methods.
  When data is unavailable, specify the needed export and deliver public findings first.
