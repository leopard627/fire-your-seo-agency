# Local SEO — Google Business Profile Audit

Check whether customers can accurately find, visit, and contact the business.
Separate eligibility, public information, and states visible only in the owner account.

## 1. Eligibility and access

- [ ] Establish business model, operating area, in-person contact, website, and profile URL.
- [ ] Check [business eligibility](https://support.google.com/business/answer/13763036?hl=en) first.
      Do not recommend profiles for online-only businesses or classes at locations the
      business is not authorized to represent. Check exceptions such as seasonal businesses;
      label unresolved cases 'eligibility unverified'.
- [ ] Distinguish storefront, service-area, and hybrid businesses before assessing addresses.
- [ ] Without manager access, use public profiles, official sites, and supplied materials.
      Public views alone do not establish ownership, verification, suspension, pending edits,
      or private performance metrics.
- [ ] Work within existing authorization. An audit alone does not authorize creating or
      claiming a profile. Complete public checks and proposed corrections without account access.

## 2. Compare the public profile with the business

Use [Google's representation guidelines](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en).

| Field | Check | Evidence to record |
|---|---|---|
| Name | Real-world branding; unnecessary location/promotional keywords | Profile/site/supplied materials |
| Address/service area | Actual base, map pin, appropriate public display | Public address/operating model |
| Categories | Core business category and necessary additional categories | Displayed category/actual service |
| Phone/website | Business contact and correct location/service page | Link destination/number comparison |
| Hours | Customer-facing hours, appointments, seasonal operations | Official notice/supplied schedule |
| Reviews/photos | Useful responses and current facilities/services | Check date/sample URLs |
| Duplicates | Possible old-name or old-location profiles | Profile URLs/differences |

- Do not publish a hidden service-area address on the website merely for consistency.
- Categories can vary by country/language; verify the available choices.
- Distinguish duplicate candidates from legitimate locations, departments, and businesses.
  Similar names or addresses alone do not justify deletion, closure, or merging.

## 3. Hours, contact, and visits

- [ ] Distinguish regular schedules from holidays/temporary changes covered by
      [special hours](https://support.google.com/business/answer/6303076?hl=en).
      Check date, weekday, and time zone.
- [ ] Do not invent regular opening hours from class schedules or appointment-only operation.
- [ ] Check that phone, website, and booking links lead to the actual business.
      Start with link and screen checks that do not place calls or create test bookings.
- [ ] Check that directions, transport/parking, services, and contact details are easy to find.
- [ ] Location pages should contain genuinely distinct location/service information.
      Do not invent branches or mass-produce pages that only swap place names.

## 4. Reviews and trust information

- [ ] Sample recent reviews for recurring questions, problems, and response accuracy.
- [ ] Follow [Google's review policy](https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114):
      invite genuine feedback neutrally, without incentives or selecting only happy customers.
- [ ] Do not create fake reviews, disparage competitors, or expose private customer details.
- [ ] Compare profile/site descriptions and photos; propose updates for obsolete information.
      Do not invent experience, qualifications, awards, ratings, or review counts.

## 5. Findings and rechecks

- [Local ranking factors](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en) include relevance,
  distance, and prominence. Profile completeness or review counts do not guarantee top placement.
- Label findings `verified / needs improvement / unverified / not applicable`, with URLs and dates.
  When supplied materials contradict public observations, do not arbitrarily choose a true value.
- Deliver corrections as `field | current value | proposed value | evidence | priority`.
  Prioritize barriers to customers such as a wrong location or unusable contact route.
- Apply authorized edits within the requested scope and check
  [edit status](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3039617?hl=en).
  Distinguish saving from publication; pending review is not a completed public change.
- Compare performance using matching region, device, and period; disclose seasonality and limits.
  Without private performance data, do not claim measured ranking, booking, or revenue gains.
